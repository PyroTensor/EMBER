# Solid Fuel Ignition and Extinction (SoFIE)

> PROXY ISS investigation summary page (EMBER team).

**Facility:** Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR) with a dedicated insert for solid-fuel tests.
**Objective:** Measure ignition delay, flame spread and extinction of solid materials across
spacecraft-relevant atmospheres, including reduced-pressure, oxygen-enriched exploration
atmospheres such as 34 % O2 / 56.5 kPa.
**Samples:** PMMA, cotton-fiberglass, Nomex and other spacecraft materials.
**Conditions:** 0g; 21-40 % O2; 48-101.3 kPa; forced flow 0-20 cm/s.
**Space benefit:** Gives the data behind material flammability limits for Artemis, Gateway and
future Mars vehicles.
