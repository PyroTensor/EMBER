# Burning and Suppression of Solids - II (BASS-II)

> PROXY ISS investigation summary page (EMBER team).

**Facility:** Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG), small flow duct.
**Objective:** Study how solid materials ignite, spread flame and extinguish in microgravity under
controlled low-speed flows, and how well suppressants work.
**Samples:** Thin PMMA, cotton-fiberglass (SIBAL) fabric, Nomex; flat sheets and rods.
**Conditions:** 0g, 101.3 kPa, 16-24 % O2, forced flow 0-50 cm/s.
**Earth benefit:** Better fire-resistant materials and fire-spread models for buildings and vehicles.
**Space benefit:** Material selection and fire response procedures (ventilation shutdown) for crewed
spacecraft.
