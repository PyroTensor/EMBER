# Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX / FLEX-2)

> PROXY ISS investigation summary page (EMBER team).

**Facility:** Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR), Multi-user Droplet Combustion Apparatus.
**Objective:** Measure how free-floating fuel droplets burn and extinguish, and test gaseous
suppressants (CO2, He) for spacecraft.
**Fuels:** n-heptane, methanol and fuel blends; droplet diameters 2-4.5 mm.
**Notable discovery:** Long-lived, nearly invisible low-temperature "cool flames" continued
after the visible hot flame went out through radiative extinction.
**Space benefit:** Suppressant selection, and awareness of fires that cameras cannot see.
